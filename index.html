I connect real-time hazard data to warnings people can act on.

The Problem

Pakistan faces recurring floods, earthquakes, landslides, and droughts — but early warnings often don't reach the people who need them in time.

Featured Work: AI-Powered Disaster Warning System

An LLM-driven system that turns raw hazard data into clear, actionable alerts — built with strict anti-fabrication rules and a structured JSON output format so every warning is consistent and trustworthy.

How It Works

1. Hazard data comes in (weather, seismic, flood sources)
2. LLaMA 3.1 (via Groq API) assesses risk and drafts the alert
3. A structured warning goes out — severity, region, and recommended action
